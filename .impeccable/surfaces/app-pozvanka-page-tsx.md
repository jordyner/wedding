# /pozvanka — invitation and preliminary RSVP

Mode: Experience (the invitation is the work), with an Operate tail (the reply card form).
Audience: invited guests on phones and desktops; job: read the invitation, answer in under a minute.
Constraints: Czech informal copy; brief-pinned world (minimalist stationery, ivory, blush); placeholders for unsupplied facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A wedding stationery suite translated to the screen: an envelope you open, an invitation card, then the small enclosed reply card. Refuses the event-landing-page arrangement (hero, nav, sections, cards grid).

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory cotton paper with fine grain, blush envelope with a sage-striped liner, a pressed blush seal with the couple's initials, deep cocoa ink, thin blush double-rule frames. Script names (Pinyon Script), everything else EB Garamond with true small caps. Shadows are soft and offset like paper on a table.

STORY: A guest sees an envelope addressed to them in spirit, opens it, reads who, when and where, then chooses to answer on a reply card tucked below, and leaves knowing a private link is in their inbox.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A soft blush-grey tabletop; centred closed envelope at roughly 85% mobile width / 440px desktop; above it "Máme pro tebe pozvánku" in small italic; the seal sits at the flap point; a quiet "Klepnutím otevři" below. Nothing else.

FORM: Brief-pinned physical-stationery world (owner-selected direction, concept roll skipped because the brief pins it). Seed key: brief-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: flap opens in 3D, the card rises out of the pocket, the envelope settles away and the full card takes its place; reply card unfolds below on "Dám vám vědět".
